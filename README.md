# Doggy Run

A retro pixel-art side-scrolling runner. Pick a Doberman, Corgi, Black & Tan Coonhound or Golden Retriever and run as far as you can.

- **Jump:** tap the screen, or press Space (Up arrow and W work too).
- **Avoid:** fire hydrants, bags of kibble, doghouses and holes. You can land on top of obstacles.
- **Climb:** floating dog-biscuit platforms are one-way, so you can jump up through them and land on top. Bones up high are worth 25 points.
- **Levels:** each park signpost starts a new level with a new look, and the scrolling speed goes up 12% (capped at level 9).
- **Other keys:** P or Esc pauses, M mutes, 1–4 pick a dog on the title screen.

Everything is in `index.html` with no build step. Open it in a browser to play.
